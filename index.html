About Our Team

Allen

Co-founder (with his wife) of the development studio Toastid Waffel and the solo-developer of its debut game, Catastrophic Inversion. He has a degree in Computer Science and works in IT.

A little about Allen:

  • He has made several small games as student projects, but Catastrophic Inversion is his first attempt at a full-feature game.
  • Occasionally teaches summer classes in introductory game design to middle/high school students.
  • He volunteers as a leader in his church's youth program.
  • His remaining free time is spent with his wife, kids, and cat.
Allen's top 5 favorite games are:
  1. Grand Theft Auto IV
  2. Halo 2
  3. Minecraft
  4. Red Matter 2
  5. Time Splitters: Future Perfect

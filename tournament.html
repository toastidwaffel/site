Event Schedule

Anticipated event times are approximate, subject to change depending on a number of factors.
Contestants should be prepared to stick around for the rest of the day just in case.

All match assignments will be randomly generated.

Round 1 - 1:00 PM
  • Deathmatch - 5 minutes per match.
  • Each contestant assigned to two 6-player matches.
  • Each player's score is combined between the two matches and the top 16 players advance to the next round.

Round 2 ~ 2:30 PM
  • Capture The Flag - 10 minutes per match.
  • Remaining 16 contestants assigned to teams of 4.
  • Teams assigned to 1 4v4 match each.
  • Winning teams are safe from elimination.
  • Players on losing teams are assigned new teams and play one more match.
  • Players on final losing team are eliminated.

Round 3 ~ 3:30 PM
  • Elimination - 10 Lives.
  • The remaining 12 contestants assigned to one 6-player match each.
  • The final 3 players in each game are safe from elimination.
  • The bottom 3 players in each game play one more match.
  • The bottom 4 players in the final match are eliminated.

Round 4 ~ 4:00 PM
  • Co-op Adventure
  • The remaining 8 contestants are assigned to 4-person teams.
  • Each team completes one adventure mode mission.
  • The team that takes the most time to complete it is eliminated.

Round 5 ~ 4:30 PM
  • Team Deathmatch - 6 minutes
  • The remaining 4 contestants are assigned to teams of 2.
  • Teams compete in one 2v2 match.
  • The losing team is eliminated.

Round 6 ~ 4:40 PM
  • Elimination - 10 Lives
  • The remaining 2 contestants face off one-on-one.
  • The last player standing wins the tournament.

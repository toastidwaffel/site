Main Gamemodes

Co-Op Adventure Mode

Work together as a team to fight enemies, navigate a multi-story building, and accomplish mission objectives.

Capture The Flag

You're split into two teams. Steal the enemy flag while defending your own. The team with the most flags at the end of the match wins.

Team Deathmatch

You're split into two teams. Kill enemy players and try to stay alive. The team with the most kills at the end of the match wins.

Free-For-All

No teams, every person for themself. Kill other players and try to stay alive. The player with the most kills at the end of the match wins.
